First Time on Court?

Quick Pickleball Rules Before You Call “Zero-Zero-Two!”

Pickleball takes five minutes to learn and a lifetime to master. Know these three core fundamentals before stepping into any Cebu open-play queue.

Standard 20ft × 44ft Court Layout 7ft Kitchen Highlighted

01. Non-Volley Zone

The Kitchen / Non-Volley Zone

The 7-foot zone on each side of the net is called The Kitchen. Players cannot hit the ball out of the air (volley) while any part of their foot touches the kitchen line or inside the zone—even if momentum carries them in after the shot.

Tip: You may step inside the Kitchen anytime to hit a ball that has already bounced!

02. Serve & Two-Bounce Rule

Serving Rules

Serves must be hit underhand below waist level (or via legal drop serve) diagonally crosscourt past the kitchen line. Under the Two-Bounce Rule, the receiving team must let the serve bounce, and the serving team must also let the return bounce before anyone can volley.

Tip: Only one serve attempt is allowed per server—a serve clipping the net that lands past the kitchen is live!

03. Why “Zero Zero Two”?

Scoring to 11

Points are scored only by the serving team. Standard open-play games go to 11 points, win by 2. In doubles, three numbers are called before every serve: Serving Score, Receiving Score, and Server Number (1 or 2). Every game starts at 0–0–2 so the opening team gets only one fault before side-out.

Tip: Even score (0, 2, 4...) serves from the right side; odd score serves from the left.
